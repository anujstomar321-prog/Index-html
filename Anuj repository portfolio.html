Projects & Assignments

01. Lipid-Based Nanocarriers for Drug Delivery

Field: Pharmaceutical Biotechnology

Objective: To understand how lipid-based nanocarriers can deliver drugs to specific sites in the body.

  • Studied concepts and basic principles of nanocarriers.
  • Explored different types and pharmaceutical applications.
  • Developed awareness of drug stability and targeted delivery.

Learning: Scientific information gathering, literature understanding, scientific writing and nanotechnology awareness.

02. Gene Editing in Livestock for Disease Resistance

Field: Genetic Engineering and Agricultural Biotechnology

Objective: To explore the potential of gene editing technologies in improving disease resistance in livestock.

  • Studied basic principles of gene editing.
  • Explored applications in animal biotechnology.
  • Reviewed future possibilities and research potential.

Learning: Genetic engineering concepts, research skills, critical thinking and presentation.

03. Biodiversity and Environmental Conservation

Field: Environmental Biotechnology

Objective: To study biodiversity, important species and conservation challenges across Indian states, including Jammu and Kashmir.

  • Collected and organised information about biodiversity.
  • Studied endangered species and conservation challenges.
  • Explored the importance of environmental protection.

Learning: Information gathering, environmental awareness, research documentation and communication.
